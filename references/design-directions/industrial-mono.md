# Direction: Industrial Mono

Instrument-panel precision. Monospace accents, dense data, quiet color, everything
aligned to a visible rhythm. Reach for this for developer tools, dashboards,
infrastructure products, technical SaaS, and audiences who trust screens that look
like they were built by people who measure things.

## Feel
Calm, exact, unglamorous in the best way. The product feels like a tool that has been
calibrated. Nothing decorative survives; every mark carries information.

## Type
A monospace with character for labels, data, and code (IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono,
Fragment Mono) paired with a neo-grotesque for headings and prose (IBM Plex Sans,
Neue Haas-class free equivalents, Geist, Public Sans). Mono is the accent, not the
body - long prose in mono is punishment. Letterspaced uppercase mono labels
(11–12px, +0.08em) are the direction's signature.

## Palette
Graphite grounds or paper-white grounds - pick one, commit. Neutral ramp with real
steps (8–10 grays). One functional accent (amber, signal green, or steel blue) plus
full semantic colors, because this direction usually ships dashboards: success/warn/
danger must read instantly and never rely on color alone (pair with icons/text). Dark
mode is legitimately at home here and may be the default *if the user agrees*.

## Shape & layout
Radius small and single (2–4px). Borders hairline (1px, low-contrast), used to
delimit, not decorate. No drop shadows; elevation via background steps. The repeated
primitive is the **panel**: titled region with a mono label header, optional metadata
right-aligned, dense content inside. Grids are strict 8px-rhythm; tables are
first-class citizens (real `<table>`, sticky headers, tabular-nums). Status chips,
keyboard-shortcut hints, and timestamps are idiomatic furniture.

## Motion
Almost none, and instant where it exists: 120–150ms fades, no entrance choreography,
no scroll effects. The one place motion earns its keep: live-data transitions
(a number ticking, a row highlight decaying over 400ms). Reduced-motion is nearly
free because there is nearly nothing to reduce.

## Imagery
Screenshots of the real product, diagrams drawn in the system's own line style,
sparklines and charts. No photography of people at laptops. Charts follow the same
palette discipline (one accent + neutrals + semantics).

## Traps
Dense is not cramped: the 8px rhythm and generous line-height keep density readable.
And resist the temptation to make it look like a terminal cosplay - green-on-black
scanline aesthetics are a different (and usually worse) direction.
