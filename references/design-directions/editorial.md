# Direction: Editorial

Magazine confidence. The content is the interface; typography does the work that other
directions delegate to cards and icons. Reach for this when reading is the product:
publications, studios, portfolios, restaurants with a story, founders with a point of view.

## Feel
Print-literate, unhurried, assured. Whitespace is generosity, not emptiness. The page
reads top to bottom like an article, not a pitch deck.

## Type
The pairing carries the direction: a characterful serif for display (Fraunces, Newsreader,
Source Serif 4, Libre Caslon) with a plain grotesque for text/UI (Söhne-class free
equivalents: Inter *in a pairing*, Public Sans, Work Sans). Display sizes are brave -
clamp(2.5rem, 8vw, 6rem) heroes are normal here. Tight leading on display (1.05–1.15),
comfortable on body (1.6–1.7). Use real italics for emphasis; this is the one direction
where they belong.

## Palette
Paper-first: warm off-whites (#FAF8F4-class) or soft cream, near-black ink (#1A1814-class),
one saturated editorial accent (oxblood, forest, cobalt, marigold) used sparingly - links,
underlines, one pull-quote rule. No gradients. Dark mode only if asked; editorial reads
best on paper tones.

## Shape & layout
Radius near zero (0–4px); rules (1px lines) instead of shadows; hanging indents,
drop caps used at most once, generous margins that widen on large screens instead of
stretching content (max measure 65–75ch). The repeated primitive is the **article
block**: kicker (small caps or letterspaced label) → display heading → standfirst →
body. Grids are asymmetric: 2/3 + 1/3, offset images, captions that behave like
footnotes. No card grids; lists are ruled lists.

## Motion
Minimal and physical: fades and small translates on section entry (250ms ease-out,
staggered 60–80ms), underline grow on link hover, nothing on scroll except lazy media.
Reading experiences are ruined by motion; restraint is the signature.

## Imagery
Full-bleed or hard-cropped photography with consistent treatment (slight desaturation
or a unifying grain). Captions always. Illustration only if hand-drawn in character.
Never icon decoration above headings.

## Traps
Do not let it collapse into "blog theme": the bravery lives in scale contrast (very
large display vs modest body) and asymmetry. If every section is centered and
same-width, the direction has failed.
