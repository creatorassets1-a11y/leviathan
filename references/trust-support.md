# Trust, Support & Honest Product Contract

User-facing products MUST expose the support and trust surfaces needed to operate them responsibly.

When applicable, generate and keep synchronized with actual behavior:
- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- cookie/tracking notice
- Acceptable Use / Community Guidelines
- refund/cancellation policy
- contact/support
- help/FAQ/knowledge base
- onboarding/feature guide
- accessibility statement
- security/trust page
- status/incident communication surface

Never fabricate testimonials, customers, logos, awards, metrics, scarcity, endorsements, or compliance claims. Pricing and cancellation must be understandable before commitment. Avoid confirm-shaming, hidden costs, forced continuity, trick questions, and other dark patterns.

Moderation/suspension systems MUST be server-side, auditable, proportionate, and provide an appeal path where appropriate.

AI-generated or AI-influenced information that users could reasonably treat as authoritative MUST be disclosed and provide appropriate uncertainty, review, correction, or escalation paths.
