# Minor Safety Mode

Activate when minors may use the product, cannot be reliably excluded, or the product is reasonably attractive to minors.

## Required analysis

- applicable age thresholds and consent requirements in target markets;
- data minimization and retention;
- profiling and targeted advertising implications;
- location collection and precision;
- public profiles and discoverability;
- direct messaging and contact from adults;
- reporting, blocking, moderation, and escalation;
- age assurance tradeoffs and false-positive/false-negative risks;
- parental controls or consent where legally required;
- emergency and safeguarding paths for high-risk products.

## Engineering floor

Do not expose precise location or sensitive child data by default. Minimize collection, restrict discoverability where appropriate, add abuse reporting, log moderation actions safely, and ensure account deletion/export behavior matches applicable retention rules.

Legal review is required when the product's audience, data, or market makes child-safety law material to the launch decision. Vibecode Max can identify risk and implementation tasks; it does not issue legal approval.
