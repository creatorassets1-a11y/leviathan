# Template: Terms of Service

Skeleton for the generated page. Formal register. Populate `[FILL]` slots from the
actual product: what it does, what is sold, which jurisdictions. Conditional sections
appear only when true of the build. This template creates strong scaffolding, not
legal advice; regulated sectors get the lawyer-review flag per
`legal-compliance.md`.

### 5. Purchases, billing, refunds *(conditional - anything selling)*
Prices and currency display, taxes, billing cycle and renewal terms for
subscriptions (with cancellation that is as easy as signup - no cancellation mazes),
refund terms [FILL, cross-reference Refund Policy; EU/UK consumer withdrawal rights
where applicable], payment processor named [FILL e.g. Stripe] and that card data goes
to the processor, not to us.

### 6. User content *(conditional - UGC)*

(scoped: host, display, transmit - not "perpetual irrevocable everything");
takedown/notice process (DMCA page cross-reference for US audience); what happens to
content on account deletion.

### 7. Intellectual property

(operator may use suggestions without obligation).

### 8. Third-party services

that their terms govern their services.

### 10. Termination
